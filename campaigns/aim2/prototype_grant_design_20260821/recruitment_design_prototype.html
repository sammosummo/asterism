Structural assumption

All-offspring/full-sibling case units. Each recruited case relative is represented as an adult offspring; two offspring of one index case are represented as full siblings. The grant permits adult children or siblings, so this composition is a simulation structure, not a settled fact about who will enrol.

Structural assumption

Family-history extension. When included, 250 additional people are represented as structurally connected, unexamined first- or second-degree pedigree candidates anchored to Acoustic participants with positive family-history flags. Candidates need not be siblings or occur one per anchor. The live reports show a route exists, but their relationships have not been normalised into a recruitable roster; neither this count nor its feasibility is an observed fact.
