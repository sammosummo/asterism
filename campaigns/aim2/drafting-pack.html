Approach → Statistical power

What the study can detect

The design was simulated as it will be run: 1,600 people in 671 family units drawn from the real Acoustic pedigrees and the family-history route, 807 of them over 65, with dementia risk set by age band rather than one rate for everybody, and about 257 cases.

Power for the test of vertical pleiotropy was established by simulation on the enrolled pedigree structure rather than by closed-form approximation, because the design contains family units of one to eight people, an age-indexed outcome rate, and ascertainment through affected probands, none of which a closed form accommodates. Two hundred replicates were simulated per condition. Under the design as currently specified, the test of ab = 0 attains 55 per cent power against a mediated effect of 0.24 with behavioural audiometry, and 46 per cent where a misclassified evoked-potential reading substitutes for it.

Type I error was measured under both branches of the union null and under the complete null, at the same replicate count as the alternatives, and no condition exceeded the nominal rate.

The design as written does not reach 80 per cent anywhere in the plausible range. Do not present 55 per cent as adequate. Present it with the design changes below, which do reach it, or state the detectable effect honestly.

The enrolment design was chosen by simulation rather than by convention. Enrolling two offspring per adjudicated case approximately doubles power relative to enrolling 250 singleton cases, despite reducing adjudications by 66, and a third offspring adds little further. Doubling the number of ascertained families raises power from 52 to 85 per cent at 2,000 participants, where recruiting proportionally more of every participant type reaches only 71 per cent at 2,369.

This follows from where the mediated signal resides. The quantity being estimated is a genetic covariance between hearing and dementia liability expressed across relatives; a case enrolled without relatives contributes almost none of it, whereas a case enrolled with two offspring contributes several informative pairs. Consistent with this, improving audiometric precision from a measurement error variance of 0.15 to 0.05 changed power by 0.065, within simulation error, because it sharpens the mediator loading rather than the path that is limiting.

Approach → Outcome measurement

Stage the outcome; do not dichotomise it

A diagnosis cuts a continuous liability in one place. The model already supposes that liability exists, so staging removes an approximation rather than adding an assumption.

The outcome will be analysed as an ordered CDR stage rather than as a binary diagnosis. The liability-threshold model underlying the mediation analysis already posits a continuous latent liability of which a diagnosis is a single thresholding; representing the outcome as five ordered stages recovers information that a single threshold discards, without altering the estimand or the population being described. In simulation on the enrolled design this raises power from 52 to 66 per cent with no additional participants and no additional adjudications, making it the least costly available improvement.

Staging is preferred to a continuous cognitive score for three reasons. First, cognitive testing is customarily administered verbally, and hearing-impaired participants underperform on such testing for reasons unrelated to dementia, which would confound the exposure with the outcome. Second, a continuous cognitive score in a population sample is dominated by normal variation whose genetic architecture differs from that of Alzheimer's disease. Third, ascertainment through affected probands contributes a constant to the log likelihood under a fixed threshold and therefore cannot bias the estimands; selection on the extreme of a continuous outcome forfeits that property.

The gain depends on the graded stages being populated. Acoustic's median age is 52, so most of that cohort will stage at zero. The measured 14-point gain assumes a questionable band comparable in size to the dementia rate. State the assumption if the figure is quoted.

CDR 0.5 is heterogeneous. Many with a questionable rating revert or prove non-Alzheimer's. Pre-specify both the five-level analysis and a collapsed 0 / 0.5 / ≥1 analysis.

Approach → Statistical methods

What has been verified about the estimator

  • Type I error. Twenty-three separate level measurements across four simulation grids, covering both branches of the union null, the complete null, the null for the direct path, and a null under each candidate design change. Every one sat at or below .025 within two Monte Carlo standard errors.
  • Ascertainment. Recruiting through an affected proband contributes exactly families × log(1/prevalence) to the log likelihood — verified at four prevalences — and a constant cannot move a maximum. Ascertainment therefore cannot bias the mediation estimand. This holds for one named proband with a threshold fixed from known prevalence.
  • Numerical integration. The family likelihood requires multivariate rectangle probabilities. The fast route was checked against quasi-Monte Carlo integration at every family size the design contains, sizes two through eight. Disagreement showed no trend with family size and reached at most 0.1 per cent of one standard error at sizes six and eight, while running 300 to 380 times faster.
  • Refusals. Fits that do not converge are counted and retained in every denominator, so a refusal counts against power rather than silently improving it. They ran at two to nine per 200 replicates.

All inference is by maximum likelihood on the joint distribution of the latent mediator and latent outcome, with the family likelihood integrated over the discrete outcome by a sequential truncation method verified against quasi-Monte Carlo integration at every family size present in the design. Type I error was measured by simulation under each null separately, including the boundary case where the mediator loading lies on its bound, for which the reference distribution is simulated under the fitted null rather than assumed. Twenty-three such measurements were made and none exceeded the nominal rate.

Approach → Limitations, and the reviewer's first question

What the analysis assumes

The genetic covariance between hearing and dementia liability is a(ab + c′). It sees only the sum of the mediated and direct paths, so genetic information alone cannot separate them. The separation comes from the residual, within-person covariance between hearing and dementia.

This is the load-bearing assumption, and it is not about pleiotropy. The model treats the entire within-person hearing–dementia association as causal. Any non-genetic factor influencing both — noise exposure, vascular disease, education, social isolation — enters the mediated path directly. This is sequential ignorability, and it is where an informed reviewer will aim first.

The design is not Mendelian randomisation. The genetics do not serve as an instrument for hearing. Do not describe it as though they do.

Identification of the mediated path rests on the residual association between hearing and dementia liability within individuals, and therefore on the assumption that this association is not confounded by factors influencing both. Covariates acting on the mediator and on the outcome are accommodated directly in the model, and adjustment for education, occupational and recreational noise exposure, and vascular risk will be pre-specified. Sensitivity of the mediated estimate to this adjustment will be reported.

The direct path

The direct path is horizontal pleiotropy: shared upstream genetic influence on dementia by a route other than measured hearing. It is recovered as the difference between a well-estimated total and a weakly-estimated product, so it is weakly identified by construction rather than for want of sample size.

The mediated claim does not require this test to succeed: mediation and pleiotropy coexist, and the test of ab = 0 held its level whether the direct path was zero or 0.2. The one route by which pleiotropy could damage the primary claim — being read as mediation — was measured directly, and the median mediated estimate rises only from 0.233 to 0.284 across the entire range of pleiotropy, about 5 per cent inflation at a plausible value.

The direct path will be reported as an estimand with a 97.5 per cent confidence set obtained by inverting the same likelihood ratio the test uses, and not as a hypothesis test with an assigned error rate. Where the profile likelihood does not fall away in either direction, the set is unbounded and the component will be reported as not separately estimable, which is a distinct statement from its being zero. Simulation confirms the direct path is estimated with little bias — median estimates of −0.02, 0.20, 0.36 and 0.66 against true values of 0, 0.2, 0.4 and 0.7 — while being poorly powered as a test, which is the basis for reporting it as an interval rather than a decision.

Internal — not for the application

What is not established

  • The combined effect of two changes is arithmetic, not measurement. Staging is 1.34× the information and doubling ascertained families 2.02×, against the 1.81× that 80 per cent requires; multiplying them to reach 83 per cent at 1,800 people assumes the two act on independent parts of the information, which was not tested.
  • Two baselines differ slightly. The effect-size curve reports 0.545 for the audiogram arm at θv = 0.24; the design-change grid reports 0.520 for its baseline. The grids differ in whether a misclassified proxy is simulated, and the gap is inside Monte Carlo error. Quote one grid or the other, not both.
  • No covariates were simulated. The model supports them and the analysis will use them, but the power figures do not include them. Covariate adjustment will change power in an unmeasured direction.
  • A small downward bias in the mediated estimate persists, around 7 per cent at two standard errors, unexplained and not attributable to ascertainment. It is most likely the small-sample behaviour of a maximum likelihood estimate of a product.
  • Between two and five fits per 200 land somewhere implausible at larger direct-path values. They inflate means without moving medians. Report medians, or handle these explicitly.
